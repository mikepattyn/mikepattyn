How I work

Ownership over the whole chain

Every product below was carried from first sketch to production by one person. That changes how fast decisions get made — and how little gets lost in translation.

  • End-to-end delivery

    Interface, API, database, cloud infrastructure — designed, built, and deployed as one coherent system. You talk to one person who understands every layer.

  • Small slices, fast learning

    I cut scope to the thinnest version that teaches us something real, ship it, and let evidence steer the roadmap — instead of betting months on a guess.

  • Quality and cost, automated

    Tests, deployment pipelines, and cost controls are set up from day one — including evals for AI features — so the pace stays honest as the product grows.

Selected work

Products, shipped and live

Not prototypes — running products with real users, each built and operated end to end.

  • barbershop.mikepattyn.nl

    Barbershop

    Dutch barbershops were juggling bookings by phone and paper. I built a multi-tenant booking platform — a customer-facing portal plus a staff dashboard — that a shop can adopt without any IT department. One codebase serves every tenant.

    Angular · .NET · DynamoDB · AWS CDK

  • flyingdarts.net

    Flyingdarts

    Real-time multiplayer darts with live video and a mobile companion that scores by voice. The hard part isn't the game — it's keeping many players in sync, live, on infrastructure that costs nearly nothing when idle. Open source.

    Angular · Flutter · .NET · WebSockets · AWS serverless

  • gofish.mikepattyn.nl

    gofish

    A location-based fishing exploration app: walk to real spots, check in, log catches, and reveal a personal map as you go. Built from scratch — geospatial backend, mobile app, and game mechanics — starting in the Schiedam region.

    Flutter · ASP.NET · PostGIS · Mapbox

  • lumen.mikepattyn.nl

    Lumen

    A gentle prompt-engineering course that teaches non-technical people to work well with AI — short lessons and a practice garden that grows with your craft. Proof that AI adoption is a teaching problem as much as a technical one.

    HTML · CSS · Vanilla JS

I also maintain open-source authentication libraries used across these products — for Flutter and for Angular. Keeping them healthy alongside everything above is a real juggling act, so if you use them — or want to — issues and pull requests are genuinely welcome. Even a small fix or a docs improvement helps more than you'd think.
